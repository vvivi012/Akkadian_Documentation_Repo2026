# About The Project

Akkadian is an extinct language spoken thousands of years ago, and it left behind a huge number of cuneiform tablets that remain untranslated even today. Because so little digital text exists in Akkadian compared to a language like English, it is a representative example of a "low-resource language," one where artificial intelligence (AI) models tend to perform much worse because it lacks the massive, digitized data required.

This website uses an Akkadian machine translation system as a case study to teach natural language processing (NLP), the field of AI focused on processing and generating human language. Rather than just building the translation system, our goal is to explain how it was built, step by step: finding and cleaning source datasets, training the model, and fine-tuning it for better accuracy.

The site includes:
- Beginner-friendly explanations of core NLP concepts
- A walkthrough of the translation pipeline used for Akkadian
- Additional resources about machine translation

Language shouldn't determine who gets access to technology. As AI becomes more central to our daily lives, the gap in AI performance between high-resource and low-resource languages risks leaving many communities behind. By making the process behind creating translation systems transparent and easy to understand, we hope to raise awareness of this digital gap and show how it can be tackled.
