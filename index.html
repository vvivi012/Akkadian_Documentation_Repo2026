About the Project

Language shouldn't determine who gets access to quality technologies. As artificial intelligence (AI) becomes more central to our daily lives, the gap in AI performance between high-resource and low-resource languages risks leaving many communities behind. This website uses an Akkadian machine translation system as a case study to teach natural language processing (NLP). Our goal is to explain how it was built: finding and cleaning source datasets, training the model, and fine-tuning it for better accuracy. By making the process behind creating translation systems for low-resource languages transparent and easy to understand, we hope to raise awareness of this digital gap and show how it can be tackled.
